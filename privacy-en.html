Privacy Policy for KörkortLätt

Last updated: 2026-08-22

Summary

  • KörkortLätt requires no account and no sign-in.
  • The app sends no data to our servers. We do not operate any servers.
  • All of your study data is stored only on your own device.
  • We do not sell personal data and we use no advertising trackers.
  • We use no analytics or crash reporting SDK.

Who is responsible

KörkortLätt is developed and provided by the app's publisher. Contact:

korkortlatt@gmail.com

What is stored on your device

KörkortLätt stores the following locally on your device, in the app's own container:

  • lesson progress
  • answer history, meaning which questions you answered correctly and incorrectly
  • spaced repetition state, meaning when a question should be reviewed again
  • settings such as language, text size, support profile and reading modes
  • study points and daily streak
  • a short technical summary if the app crashes or freezes, delivered by iOS's own diagnostics (MetricKit). It contains an error code, the app version and the iOS version — nothing about you or what you studied. You can read and delete it under Settings → Voice, support and app info → Problem reports, and it is never sent anywhere unless you copy it into an email yourself.

This data is used only to make the app work and to adapt the learning flow on that device. It never leaves the device through the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or store on our own servers:

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • location
  • contacts
  • photos
  • payment information
  • health data
  • advertising identifiers or other tracking identifiers

Network

The app contains no networking code of its own and makes no calls to any server we operate. The only times your device reaches the internet because of this app are:

1. When the App Store fetches or manages your subscription. This is handled by Apple.

2. When you tap a link to an official source such as Transportstyrelsen or Trafikverket. That page then opens in your browser and that site's own privacy policy applies.

Text-to-speech

Read-aloud uses Apple's built-in speech synthesis in iOS. We do not send the text to any external service. How iOS handles speech synthesis is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and your iOS settings.

Purchases and subscription

KörkortLätt Premium is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through the App Store. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple.

  • The app never receives, sees or stores your payment details.
  • We receive no personal information about you through the purchase.
  • We can see aggregated and anonymised sales statistics in App Store Connect.
  • Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase itself.

You manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple account settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all saved data.
  • Or by uninstalling the app, which removes everything the app has stored on the device.

Because we store no data about you, you do not need to contact us to be erased.

Children

The app is aimed at people studying for a Swedish category B driving licence. The app collects no data about the user and has no social features, no chat and no user-generated content.

Your rights under GDPR

Because we do not collect or process personal data about you, there is no data held by us to access, correct or delete. Data you create in the app resides on your device and is entirely under your control.

Changes

If the app begins collecting data in the future, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the App Store privacy labels will be updated at the same time.
